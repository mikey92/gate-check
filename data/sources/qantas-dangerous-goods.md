Carry-on only: Power banks and spare batteries must be packed in your carry-on, not your checked baggage. ... Keep them within reach: Keep them within reach during the flight, in your seat pocket or under the seat in front of you. Two per person: You can carry up to two power banks per person, each not exceeding 160Wh. No charging or use in flight: Power banks cannot be used or charged during the flight, including via in-seat USB ports.
