In response to the new lithium battery -powered power bank specifications issued by the International Civil Aviation Organization, EVA Air has revised the specifications for carrying power banks in carry-on baggage as : Effective from March 31, 2026, each person is allowed to carry a maximum of 2 power banks on board.
