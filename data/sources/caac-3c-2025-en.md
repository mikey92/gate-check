Recently, to ensure aviation safety, CAAC issued an urgent notice prohibiting passengers from carrying power banks without CCC markings, with unclear CCC markings, or from recalled models/batches on domestic flights starting June 28, 2025.

Statements quoted from this page:

- Certification: "CAAC issued an urgent notice prohibiting passengers from carrying power banks without CCC markings, with unclear CCC markings, or from recalled models/batches on domestic flights starting June 28, 2025."
- Effective date: "starting June 28, 2025"
