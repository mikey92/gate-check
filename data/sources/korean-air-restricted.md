Rules apply in accordance with the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations. Travel regulations (Carry-on allowed , checked baggage prohibited ) : The use and charging of power banks on board are strictly prohibited. | Lithium battery capacity | Power bank | Spare battery | 100 Wh or less | 2 per person (Over 100 Wh and up to 160 Wh requires airline approval) | 5 per person (If you are carrying more than 6 batteries, you must visit the check-in counter for airline approval) | Over 100 Wh and up to 160 Wh | 2 per person (Airline approval is required, and if carrying power banks between 100 Wh and 160 Wh, a combined total of up to 2 is permitted) | Over 160 Wh | Carriage not permitted | To prevent short circuits, batteries must be placed individually in transparent plastic bags or with terminals wrapped with electrical tape when carrying on board. Storing them in the overhead bin is strictly prohibited.
