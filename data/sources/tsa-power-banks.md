Power Banks. Carry On Bags: Yes. Checked Bags: No. Portable chargers or power banks containing a lithium ion battery must be packed in carry-on bags. Spare lithium batteries, which include both power banks and phone chargers, are prohibited in checked luggage. For more information, see the FAA guidance on portable rechargers. Last Updated on May 14, 2025. The final decision rests with the TSA officer on whether an item is allowed through the checkpoint.
