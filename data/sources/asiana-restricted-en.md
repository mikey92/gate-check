Spare lithium batteries and power banks for charging are restricted in checked baggage. Please carry these on to the cabin. Batteries must be insulated with tape or placed in a transparent zip-lock bag (one per battery) and must be carried on your person. It is strictly prohibited to store them in the overhead bin. You cannot use or charge power banks or e-cigarettes on board.

Statements quoted from this page:

- Limit without approval: "Lithium-ion 100Wh or less, Lithium-metal 2g or less | 2 | Prohibited | 5 | Prohibited"
- Limit with approval: "Lithium-ion over 100Wh to 160Wh or less (Airline approval required) | 2 *NOTE : If you are carrying power banks between 100Wh and 160Wh, a total of up to two is permitted."
- Using it in flight: "You cannot use or charge power banks or e-cigarettes on board."
