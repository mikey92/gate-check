The ICAO’s updated safety guidance, effective 27 March 2026, introduces the first coordinated international standard on portable battery limits in aviation. ... Maximum two power banks per passenger, carried in the cabin only — never in checked baggage. Each power bank must not exceed 100 watt-hours (Wh) without airline approval. Power banks between 100 Wh and 160 Wh require prior airline approval. Anything above 160 Wh is banned entirely. No charging power banks during flight. ... No using power banks to charge devices during flight. ... Power banks must be stored in your seat pocket or under the seat. Overhead bin storage is prohibited so crew can spot and respond to overheating. The IATA’s guidance to operators reinforces these rules ... British Airways follows the ICAO baseline. Power banks between 100 Wh and 160 Wh require operator approval for each passenger. ... FAQ: Yes, but you’re limited to two power banks (or one on Emirates and Southwest), each under 100 Wh, carried in the cabin only.
