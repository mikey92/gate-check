Carry-on regulations () *Prohibited in checked baggage | Category | Power bank | Spare battery | 100Wh or below | UP TO 2 (100Wh to 160Wh requires airline approval) | UP TO 5 | Over 100Wh and up to 160Wh | UP TO 2 (Airline approval required) | Over 160Wh | Prohibited | Note : Up to two power banks and spare batteries with capacities between 100Wh and 160Wh are allowed to be transported. Storage method: The use or charging of power banks or e-cigarettes on board is strictly prohibited. Please do not store power banks or e-cigarettes in the overhead bins. Instead, keep them with you or place them in the seat pocket in front of you. Batteries must be individually protected to prevent short circuits.
