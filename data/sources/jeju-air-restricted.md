Portable charger: Up to 160Wh: Up to 2 items are allowed in the cabin. Over 100Wh and up to 160Wh: Airline approval is required. Power banks must be protected against short circuits and must either be carried on your person or kept in a visible location in the cabin. ... For routes departing from Thailand, transportation may be restricted if the power bank exceeds 32,000mAh. The use and charging of power banks and e-cigarettes are strictly prohibited in the cabin. ... Power Banks | ≤ 160Wh(43,000mAh) | Carry-on (2 pieces) | > 160Wh(43,000mAh) | Prohibited for transport ... For the safety of the aircraft, the use and charging of power banks are prohibited during the flight. (However, use and charging are permitted when necessary for medical purposes such as using a POC) Portable chargers exceeding 100Wh but not exceeding 160Wh require airline approval and must be presented at the check-in counter.
