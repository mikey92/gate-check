Video: Traveling with Lithium Batteries (30-second version). Lithium-ion batteries are allowed based on watt hours (Wh). Batteries 0-100 Wh are allowed on passenger aircraft, 101-160 Wh require an air carrier’s approval, and batteries exceeding 160 Wh are forbidden. ... SAFO 25002 Managing the Risks of Lithium Batteries Carried by Passengers and Crewmembers: This SAFO highlights the risks associated with the carriage of lithium batteries in aircraft passenger compartments.

Statements quoted from this page:

- Limit without approval: "Batteries 0-100 Wh are allowed on passenger aircraft,"
- Limit with approval: "101-160 Wh require an air carrier’s approval, and batteries exceeding 160 Wh are forbidden."
