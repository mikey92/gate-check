Travelling with power banks. For safety reasons, strict regulations apply to the use and storage of lithium battery power banks on all Cathay Pacific flights. Each passenger may carry a maximum of two lithium battery power banks. Under Hong Kong law, passengers are prohibited from: Charging power banks via in-seat power sources; Using power banks to charge or power their personal electronic devices. Additionally, power banks must not be placed in checked baggage or overhead compartments under any circumstances; they must remain securely stored in cabin baggage under the seat in front of the passenger. ... Lithium batteries with no or unclear marking of Watt-hour (Wh) rating or Lithium Content (LC) will be refused carriage.
