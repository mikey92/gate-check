Lithium and sodium-ion batteries can be carried by air depending on configuration and Watt-hour rating (for rechargeable) or lithium content (for non-rechargeable). In addition, spare batteries are not allowed in checked baggage.

Statements quoted from this page:

- Carry-on or checked: "In addition, spare batteries are not allowed in checked baggage."
- Using it in flight: "Also, be aware that, although you may be allowed to carry these items, their use on board the aircraft may be prohibited or at least limited. Check with your airline for further details."
