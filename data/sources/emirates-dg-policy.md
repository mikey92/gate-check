Power banks: Usage of power banks to charge any electronic devices onboard and charging the power bank itself using the onboard power source is prohibited. A power bank is classified as any device whose primary purpose is to charge or power other electronic equipment. Passengers are allowed to carry one power bank with a maximum capacity of 100 watt hour, with the power bank containing clearly visible capacity rating information. Power banks are permitted in cabin baggage only and not in checked-in baggage. Onboard the aircraft, power banks cannot be placed in the overhead compartment and must be securely placed either in in the seat pocket or under the seat in front of you to ensure quick access and safety compliance.
